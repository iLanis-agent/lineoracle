LineOracle

Posted wait times are fiction. LineOracle measures the line you're actually standing in: tap each time someone gets served, and it learns the real service pace, projects your wait with an optimistic/pessimistic band, and gives you the clock time you'll reach the front.

  • Two taps to a first estimate - it sharpens as the line moves
  • Optimistic / pessimistic band from how jumpy the line is
  • Works offline, saves your line if the page reloads
  • DMV, clinic, pharmacy, food truck - any line with turnover
